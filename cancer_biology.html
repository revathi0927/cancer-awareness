Biology of Cancer

Understanding How Cancer Develops and Spreads

Definition

Cancer is a group of diseases in which abnormal cells grow uncontrollably and crowd out normal healthy cells in the body. These abnormal cells develop because of changes or mutations in genes (DNA), which control how cells grow, divide, and function.

Normally, cells grow in a controlled way and die when they become damaged or old, but cancer cells continue to survive and multiply.

Small fact: The word “cancer” comes from the Greek word “karkinos,” meaning crab.

What Causes Cancer?

Cancer begins when the normal process of cell division goes wrong. These changes may happen because of inherited genetic mutations, harmful chemicals, radiation, smoking, unhealthy lifestyle habits, or sometimes without any clear reason.

Small fact: Scientists have found evidence of cancer even in ancient fossils.

Types of Genes

🧬
Proto-oncogenes
Proto-oncogenes help normal cells grow and divide. When these genes mutate, they can become oncogenes and cause uncontrolled cell growth.
🛡️
Tumor Suppressor Genes
Tumor suppressor genes normally stop cells from dividing too quickly. When damaged, cells may grow uncontrollably.
🔧
DNA Repair Genes
DNA repair genes help fix damaged DNA. When these genes fail to work properly, mutations can build up and increase cancer risk.
Small fact: Cancer usually develops after multiple genetic changes over time.

Types of Cancer

Cancer can be classified based on the type of cells where it begins.

  • Carcinomas – Start in skin or organ linings
  • Sarcomas – Begin in bones or connective tissues
  • Blood cancers – Affect blood and bone marrow
Small fact: There are more than 100 different types of cancer.

Cancer Stages

Low Stage (Stage 1–2)
Cancer is still small and has not spread very far.

High Stage (Stage 3–4)
Cancer has spread further into nearby tissues or other body parts.

How Cancer Spreads

Cancer cells can break away from the original tumor and travel through the blood or lymphatic system to different parts of the body. This process is called metastasis.

Why Understanding Cancer Matters

Different cancers behave differently. Some grow slowly while others spread rapidly. Understanding the biology of cancer helps doctors choose treatments such as surgery, chemotherapy, radiation therapy, or immunotherapy.
