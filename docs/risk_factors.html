Cancer Risk Factors

Understanding What Increases Cancer Risk

Major Risk Factors

Tobacco

Tobacco use is one of the leading causes of cancer worldwide. Cigarettes and tobacco products contain harmful chemicals that damage DNA and increase the risk of cancers such as lung, mouth, throat, and bladder cancer.

Long-term smoking and exposure to secondhand smoke can seriously affect health and increase cancer risk.

Reproductive Factors

Certain reproductive and hormonal factors may affect breast cancer risk because hormones influence breast cell growth.

  • Early start of periods may increase hormone exposure
  • Late menopause may increase cancer risk
  • Late pregnancy or not having children may affect hormone levels
  • Higher hormone exposure may increase abnormal cell growth

Alcohol

Alcohol consumption increases the risk of cancers such as liver, mouth, throat, breast, and oesophagus cancer.

Alcohol can damage body cells and affect the body’s ability to repair DNA properly.

Occupational Exposure

Some people may be exposed to harmful substances in workplaces that increase cancer risk.

  • Industrial chemicals and asbestos
  • Dust, metals, and pollution
  • Radiation exposure
  • Certain factory or industrial jobs

Obesity

Obesity is linked to several cancers including colon, kidney, breast, uterus, and pancreatic cancer.

Healthy eating, regular exercise, and maintaining a healthy weight can help lower cancer risk.

Radiation

High-energy radiation such as X-rays and ultraviolet rays can damage DNA and increase cancer risk.

  • Too much sunlight exposure may cause skin cancer
  • Medical radiation is usually safe when used properly
  • Protective measures help reduce radiation risks

Quick Summary

Tobacco is one of the biggest preventable causes of cancer.
Hormones and reproductive factors may influence breast cancer risk.
Alcohol can increase cancer risk, especially with long-term use.
Exposure to harmful workplace substances may increase cancer risk.
Healthy lifestyle choices can help reduce many cancer risks.
